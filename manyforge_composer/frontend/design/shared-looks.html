Shared looks: notices, warnings, errors and section headers

UI6-I-VC step VC4. Each row shows one message that exists in the Composer today, rendered with its real classes on the left and with the proposed shared look on the right. Only the look changes: text, placement, role and severity stay as they are. The proposed look reuses the workbench notification colors, so a warning is the same amber box wherever it appears.

Three shared classes replace about 25 separate looks: notice (a box, with a band variant under headers and a floating variant over canvases), inline-message (text only) and hint (muted help text). Each takes severity-info, severity-warning or severity-error. Inside the central inspector, boxes follow its compact spacing tokens.

Section headers

The node catalog's category header is the reference. The Program inspector and Viewer tools headers already share its text, tint and accent bar, but are taller, with more padding and larger buttons. The proposal gives them the catalog's measurements; without a category they keep the default accent.
