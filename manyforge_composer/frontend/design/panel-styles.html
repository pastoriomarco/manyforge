Panel styles: readability proposals

The side panels are reproduced from the live app (Program settings, Parameters, Viewer settings, Robot control, Selected object, Scene hierarchy, Deployment). Each is rendered in three coherent styles close to today's colours. Content is unchanged; only hierarchy, spacing, separators and type change. Pick one style, or mix a rule from another.

Rules shared by all three

  • Three text levels only: section title, label (muted), value.
  • One separator kind per style, used everywhere; no stray hairlines.
  • One control height (1.9rem) and text size for inputs, selects and buttons.
  • Labels above fields (A, B) or in a left column (C, D): one third for the label, two thirds for the field. Long labels are shortened where the section gives the context, and wrap to two lines otherwise.
  • Actions at the end of their section, primary first; destructive last. Buttons share their row and fill it, wrapping when they do not fit.
  • Scope tags (Installation, Program, Session) on section or sub-headings only.

A · Headed sections

  • Tinted header strip per section with a small-caps title.
  • The strips are the only separators; content sits on the panel ground.
  • Closest to today's look; clearest "where does this section start".

B · Cards

  • Each section is a card; list rows are small filled tiles.
  • Most separation and whitespace; slightly less content per screen.

D · Cards + property grid

  • Sections are cards with a tinted title bar; inside, label | value rows on hairlines.
  • C's density and alignment with B's separation between sections.

C · Property grid

  • Collapsible groups (chevron), label | value rows on hairlines.
  • Densest and most aligned; best for long settings and inspectors.
